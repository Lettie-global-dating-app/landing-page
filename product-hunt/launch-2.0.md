## Description (260자 이내)

> Lettie is a penpal dating app where letters really take time. You write tonight, watch the envelope fly across a dotted globe, and it lands tomorrow. AI translates between 28 languages in a tap, and every country you reach gives you a stamp. Slow letters, real people.

## 메이커 첫 댓글

> Hi Product Hunt — maker here. 👋
>
> Two months ago we launched Lettie 1.0 here. Since then we rebuilt the whole app around one rule: **a letter should take time.**
>
> **What's new in 2.0**
> - ✉️ **Letters fly.** Your letter physically crosses a globe and lands hours later — sometimes a day. You can watch it go.
> - 🌐 **AI translation, 28 languages.** Write in yours, they read in theirs. The demo in the video is a real letter about Hanoi rain.
> - 🏷️ **Stamps.** Every country you reach gives you a stamp. People started writing to Kenya and Finland just to fill the album.
> - 🧑‍🎨 **Pixel characters instead of selfies.** You can draw your own.
> - 🌍 **Discover.** Open strangers' envelopes, three at a time, a few times a day.
>
> **Why slow?** When a message takes 14 hours to arrive, people write differently — longer, kinder, more honest. Instant messaging killed the first message; we're trying to bring it back.
>
> It's free on iOS and Android, 150+ countries. I'll be here all day — tell me what you'd write first, and what you'd want us to build next.
